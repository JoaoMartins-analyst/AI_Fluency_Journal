### Things I struggled with

I initially did not know what remained untested or how much I could conclude from five examples.

My first conclusion was too broad: these results do not establish that the assistant will reliably follow instructions in every situation.

### AI explanation and feedback

ChatGPT reviewed my written summaries and explained:

- Why the ticket-age answer was an unnecessary-uncertainty failure.
- Why I should evaluate additional details as well as the main answer.
- Why five tests provide limited evidence about overall reliability.
- How diligence includes responsibility for the final output as well as disclosure of AI involvement.

### What I learned

- AI can help with creative exploration and structured explanations.
- Existing subject knowledge makes it easier to evaluate an answer.
- Related statements in a source can resolve an apparent ambiguity.
- An assistant can be overly cautious even when the source supports a clear answer.
- Paraphrases, boundary cases, repeated runs and longer conversations remain untested.
- Future test logs should preserve the prompt, relevant source passage and exact response.
