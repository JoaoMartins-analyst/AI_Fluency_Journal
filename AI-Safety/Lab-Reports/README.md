\# AI Safety Lab Reports



This folder is for reports from authorised AI safety exercises and controlled practice environments.



Topics will include prompt injection, instruction handling, information disclosure and the evaluation of safeguards.



Each report should describe:



\- The lab or environment and its permitted scope.

\- The behaviour being investigated.

\- The procedure and test inputs.

\- The observed results and supporting evidence.

\- Any mitigation tested.

\- Remaining limitations and lessons learned.



These reports document my learning and the results of specific exercises.
